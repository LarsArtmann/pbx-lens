### Release hardening (2026-10-26)

- The duplication gate moved into the pre-commit hook (pin-enforcing,
  self-tested in CI) after catching this release's own features.
- The load gate that arms release receipts can no longer arm on a hot
  15-minute average hidden behind a quiet 1m (string-compare bug its
  own receipt exposed; the case is seeded red in its self-test).
- CDR ingest survives a bare-semicolon formatting line (found by the
  parser property test; replayed as a committed regression seed), and
  the Telnyx client gained a response-header timeout so a stalled
  upstream cannot hang the poller.
