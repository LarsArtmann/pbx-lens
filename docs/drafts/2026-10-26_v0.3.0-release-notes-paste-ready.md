## v0.3.0 — the analytics surface, transcript truth, and gate honesty

Two days of north-star work: the dashboard became an analytics surface,
search learned what was SAID, every number got a page, and the gates
learned to prove themselves — including catching their own author.

### Analytics (read-only, denominator-honest)

- Spend card: mean cost per billed minute over PRICED calls only; mixed
  currencies refuse to sum; week-over-week delta.
- Segment tables: top PBX-side numbers and outside parties with AI share,
  average duration, and per-segment cost rate — and every top number now
  opens its own trend page (`/segments/{number}`) rendering the same
  aggregates the dashboard uses, so the two can never disagree.
- Quality: hangup causes, no-user-turn share with a week-over-week trend
  in percentage points, per-call truth signals (no user turn, self-dial,
  recording tail).
- Anomaly baseline: the density heatmap carries a weekday note — today
  against that weekday's usual ±2σ band, silent until two weeks of
  history.
- Counterpart history on the call detail page.
- Ops honesty: `/api/ops` now carries an index-coverage table (per source:
  raw indexed rows, oldest/newest span, newest-row age, a `stale` flag
  against a 48-hour SLO) — "is the index drying up" has an answer.
- CDR calls derive direction from the PBX sides; the export streams and
  the recording proxy carry Server-Timing (`firstpage` names the
  time-to-first-row; the recording names both upstream hops).

### Search and views

- Transcript mode (`?mode=transcript`): one ranked hit per matching TURN,
  anchored by turn index, deep-linking into the highlighted turn — search
  now answers "what was SAID", not just "which call".
- FTS5 search over transcripts and phone digits (any 4+ digit run), with
  the match log and deep links.
- Saved views: the calls page records your filtered views as chips
  (localStorage, five most recent), and dashboard/segment pages carry a
  canonical Copy link — shareable URLs without volatile params.

### Catalog and platform

- The federated eventcatalog renders the real topology: FreeSWITCH and
  Telnyx as read-only external systems, the call-index service with its
  read/write edges, the receiver webhook as a source container.
- templ-components v1.24.0 with one formatting dialect; lint at zero.
- go-cqrs-lite wave: systemscenario-backed end-to-end and chaos tests,
  the golden payload trail, optimistic-concurrency and delayed-sqlite
  proofs.
- Ingest hardening: `-dry-run` everywhere, `-max-age` retention gating,
  the `refresh` backfill classifier; the idempotent replay hop has a
  published number (≈5.1 µs/row vs 6.4 fresh) and the parsers are
  race-clean and fuzz-proven.

### Fixed

- Six CI-red lint findings fixed at root (segment cost rate divides by
  the domain-owned constant, quality-trend seed builds by append, two
  optional-result nil returns carry reasoned suppressions).
- styles.css regenerated by the pinned nix builder (the previous
  release's analytics utilities had shipped without their CSS).
- The dashboard's freshness pill no longer fights HTTP caching.
- docs no longer claim phantom tests — a new gate forces every
  backticked test name in living docs to exist in the tree.

### Gates that prove themselves (the release's own war story)

- The art-dupl zero-group duplication gate caught the session's own
  features three pushes deep; the clones were extracted, and the gate
  moved left into the pre-commit hook — refusing to run on any binary
  but the CI pin, self-tested red in CI on every push.
- The load gate that arms the FULL receipt had never refused a hot 15m
  average hidden behind a quiet 1m (a string-compare bug its own
  receipt exposed); it now seeds that exact case red.

### Release hardening (2026-10-26)

- The duplication gate moved into the pre-commit hook (pin-enforcing,
  self-tested in CI) after catching this release's own features.
- The load gate that arms release receipts can no longer arm on a hot
  15-minute average hidden behind a quiet 1m (string-compare bug its
  own receipt exposed; the case is seeded red in its self-test).
- CDR ingest survives a bare-semicolon formatting line (found by the
  parser property test; replayed as a committed regression seed), and
  the Telnyx client gained a response-header timeout so a stalled
  upstream cannot hang the poller.
